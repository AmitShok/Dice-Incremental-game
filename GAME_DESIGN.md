# Game design

## North star
A cozy die-rolling table becomes an engine for controlling probability. Gamblers Table informs object accumulation, earned helpers and build-changing progression; artwork, character design and mechanics here are original.

## Current loop
One free Ivory D6, zero cash. Click a die, or Space/roll-table for a batch. A roll includes anticipation, lift, tumble, landing, revealed face, sound and payout. Cash buys dice, upgrades, helpers and per-family automatic rolling. Dragging sets logical spatial position. There is a 100-die table cap and eight-helper cap.

The free starter replaces the previous $10/empty-table opening deliberately, avoiding an initial shop action before the first satisfying roll. Its ownership counts toward the D6 cost curve; the second D6 costs $20. Existing D6/D20/Golden price bases remain.

## Mechanical identity
Lucky changes probability. Ember explodes on maxima with a depth cap. Prism boosts other dice within 115 logical pixels by 50%; multiple Prism auras do not stack. Loaded corners doubles upper-half weights for ordinary D6. Lucky sixes triples natural six payouts. Table harmony enables doubles ×2, triples ×5 and a 1–5 straight ×8. Highest combination wins, and its multiplier affects that roll batch. Independently scheduled helper/automatic rolls are not one combined batch.

Helpers walk to ready, non-automated dice with round-robin selection. Automatic families continue operating while helpers work on other dice. Manual play remains available.

## Fate
Name explicitly chosen by the user. At $100K run earnings, reset money, dice, ordinary upgrades, automation and helpers; receive floor(sqrt(run earnings / 100000)) Fate. Every lifetime Fate grants +10% base income. Spendable Fate buys four Resource-defined talents; talent ranks, total Fate, settings, achievements and lifetime statistics survive. A confirmation describes exactly what resets.

## Onboarding and accessibility
Contextual guidance explains first roll, first purchase and combination unlock. Hover describes dice. Motion, counter flashes, floating payouts, particles and sounds are independently switchable, with volume control. Space rolls all available dice. Focusable shop buttons support keyboard navigation. Screen-reader support, remapping and localization remain release tasks.

## Not implemented by design
Crafting, fusion, multiple rooms, challenge runs, minigames, multiple prestige layers, machines and Steam integrations remain future content. No imitation characters, ripped sprites, copied UI artwork or reference audio.
